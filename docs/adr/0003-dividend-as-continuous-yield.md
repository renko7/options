# ACME's dividend is priced as a continuous yield, though the Course names a discrete ex-date

The chain, the Tools and every Worked Example price ACME's $0.50 quarterly dividend as a 2% continuous yield, while the Course also gives it a real-looking ex-dividend date (Dec 10, 2026) for exercise and assignment Lessons. A continuous yield keeps one Black-Scholes model behind every number, so the chain, the Tools and the Lessons agree. Repricing with the discrete dividend would mean switching the whole chain to an escrowed-dividend or tree model. Lessons that depend on the discrete dividend (early exercise, dividend risk) price it explicitly with the binomial tree and say so. The mismatch is labelled where it matters (conversions, parity), and the Advanced Track's pricing Module treats discrete dividends properly.
